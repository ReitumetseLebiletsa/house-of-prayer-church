About Our Church

HOUSE OF PRAYER CHURCH OF ALL NATIONS, known as Arena of Solutions, is a Christian ministry based in Qalakheng, Mohale's Hoek, Lesotho.

We are committed to prayer, the teaching of God's Word, spiritual growth, fellowship and the transformation of lives through faith in Jesus Christ.

Our doors are open to people seeking God, encouragement, spiritual guidance and a deeper relationship with Him.
