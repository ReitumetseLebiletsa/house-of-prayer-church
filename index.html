Welcome to Our Church

A home of faith, prayer and transformation

Welcome to House of Prayer Church of All Nations, Arena of Solutions. We are a house of prayer committed to serving God, building faith, transforming lives and bringing hope to our communities.

We believe in the power of prayer, the Word of God, healing, deliverance and the transforming presence of Jesus Christ. Whether you are visiting for the first time or returning home, there is a place for you here.

Join us as we grow together in faith and fulfil the purpose God has placed in our lives.
