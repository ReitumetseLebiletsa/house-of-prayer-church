Who We Are

A House of Prayer for All Nations

HOUSE OF PRAYER CHURCH OF ALL NATIONS is a Christian ministry based in Qalakheng, Mohale's Hoek, Lesotho.

We believe in the power of prayer, the Word of God and the transforming presence of Jesus Christ. Our desire is to see people experience spiritual growth, healing, deliverance and a renewed understanding of their purpose.

We welcome people from all walks of life to grow in faith, fellowship with one another and seek God together.
